— 01 · OVERVIEW

The most expensive report isn't the one that takes hours. It's the one that's a month late.

Today, answering a simple question — how are complaints trending against last year? — means someone exports from SmartSolve, cleans it in Excel, builds a chart, and pastes it into a deck. Next month, they do it again. The data was right there the whole time; the work is in the retrieval.

One governed home for that data changes the default. SmartSolve stays the system of record — nothing about how your team logs complaints, NCs, or CAPAs changes. But every night, the data lands in one governed place, modeled once, so downstream reporting can refresh automatically instead of being rebuilt each month.

The hours are real — but they're not the risk. The risk is cadence. When trending is a month-end exercise, a complaint signal can sit unseen for thirty days before anyone charts it. The bigger risk isn't generating the report — it's recognizing a signal later than you could have. A nightly refresh turns how many NCs did we open yesterday? into a question with an answer this morning — and turns "show me your complaint trending" from a two-week scramble into something you pull up during the inspection.

— 02 · INTEGRATION

SmartSolve to dashboards: a well-worn path, not a science project.

SmartSolve stores structured quality records — complaints, nonconformances, CAPAs, audits, change control, training. The integration reads them on a nightly schedule — read-only, no writes back to SmartSolve — checks the data on every load, and organizes it into governed, documented quality data ready for dashboards and reporting, in Power BI or the analytics tools you already use.

Whether you already have a data platform or you're starting with SmartSolve and Excel, the approach can fit your existing environment and IT standards — anything new gets built with your IT team, not around them.
